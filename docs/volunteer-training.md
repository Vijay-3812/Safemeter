# Volunteer training module (15 minutes)

Volunteers are **first responders, not medics**. The goal is to reach the rider quickly, make
the scene safe, get professional help moving, and avoid causing harm.

## 1. Before you accept

- Only accept if you can actually go **now** and reach the location within ~10 minutes.
- If you are driving, pull over before using the app.
- Tap **Navigate** — the pin is the last GPS fix from the vehicle; the rider may be a few metres
  away (thrown or moved to the roadside).

## 2. Arriving — make the scene safe

1. Park well clear, hazard lights on, do not stop in a live lane.
2. Look for traffic, fuel leaks, fire, downed power lines. Your safety first.
3. Ask bystanders to warn oncoming traffic (phone torch, raised hands) 50 m back.

## 3. Call for help

- **Call 108** (ambulance) immediately if it hasn't been called. Give the Google Maps location
  from the app, the number of injured, and whether the person is conscious and breathing.
- 112 is the unified emergency number; 100 for police.
- Stay on the line; tell the operator what changes.

## 4. The rider

- Talk to them. Say who you are and that help is coming.
- **Do NOT move them** unless there is immediate danger (fire, traffic). Assume a neck or spinal
  injury after any fall from a moving vehicle.
- **Do NOT remove the helmet** unless they are not breathing and you cannot open the airway
  otherwise. Loosen the strap if it helps breathing.
- If they are unconscious but breathing, keep them as they are, support the head, monitor
  breathing. If they are not breathing and you are CPR-trained, start CPR.
- Heavy bleeding: press firmly with a clean cloth, keep pressing.
- Keep them warm and still; do not give food or water.

## 5. Hand over

- When the ambulance arrives, tell them what you saw: speed at impact from the app, how the
  rider was found, how long they've been there, anything that changed.
- Tap **I have arrived** in the app when you reach the scene — this records the response time
  the project needs.
- The rider's family may call the number on the alert — be factual and calm.

## 6. Legal protection

India's Good Samaritan law (Supreme Court guidelines 2016, Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019,
Section 134A) protects people who help road accident victims in good faith from civil or criminal
liability and from being compelled to be witnesses. You are not required to give your name at
the hospital.

## 7. Quick card

```
SAFE   → park clear, hazards on, check traffic / fuel / fire
CALL   → 108 ambulance, share the map link
STAY   → don't move them, don't remove the helmet, talk to them
STOP   → bleeding: firm pressure
HAND   → tell the ambulance crew what you know; tap "I have arrived"
```
